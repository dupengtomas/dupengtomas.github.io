Peng Du (杜鹏)

Researcher, Zhejiang University
Research Interests: CAD, Embodied Intelligence

Biography

Dr. Peng Du is a researcher at the State Key Lab of CAD & CG, Zhejiang University. He previously worked at Huawei as Chief Expert of Ascend CANN ecosystem and led the HiSilicon Robotics Lab. His research focuses on CAD modeling, embodied AI, and intelligent robotics.
